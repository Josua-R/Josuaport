The order - Term and Steps

From start to final delivery.

  1. 1

    Send your brief

    Describe what you want and include references (video/photo), design sheet (if available) and format requirement/intended usage.

    *You can pick one of my portfolio pieces for more specific start.

  2. 2

    Quote & confirmation

    I’ll reply as soon as possible to confirm the work, price, and delivery estimate.

    *30% upfront payment are required before starting on the work.

  3. 3

    Creation & revisions

    I create your artwork and share progress.

    *Free revision are limited to 2 during the rough phase and 1 minor revision during finishing.
    *For Additional revision during rough phase, it will cost 30% of the total price. Paid upfront.
    *For major revision during finishing, full price of the order are imposed. Paid upfront.

  4. 4

    Final delivery

    After approval, you receive the completed work.

    *Final preview will be provided, then full payments are required before final delivery of the work.


I don't provide any refund for the services I offered. Please keep it in mind when you're ordering. Thank you.
